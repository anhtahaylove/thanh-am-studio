Source of runtime/python/Lib/site-packages/_soundfile_data/libsndfile_x64.dll, as bundled in the soundfile 0.14.0
wheel (DLL sha256 22518c16f9d13eda5ae5adf999c4740d7d16cc2d6b178b36dd4ac2f759a38559).

soundfile 0.14.0 (https://github.com/bastibe/python-soundfile/tree/0.14.0) takes the DLL from its _soundfile_data
submodule, https://github.com/bastibe/libsndfile-binaries at commit a3e6f9769d0c7e91d2d036cf0fdbe5b4bbf18b87. The DLL
was added there in commit 1ac5f9412c ("Build Windows libsndfile dynamically, but dependencies statically",
2024-12-30) by .github/workflows/build-libs.yml: `vcpkg install libsndfile:x64-windows-custom
--overlay-triplets=triplets` on the windows-2019 runner, with libsndfile built as a DLL and its dependencies linked
in statically (triplets/x64-windows-custom.cmake).
